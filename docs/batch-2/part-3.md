## Session detail

### Session 1 — Project mapping and baseline development *(24 September)*

Defining the project footprint and area of influence, assembling the project geo-profile, and generating a baseline report in the eToolkit. Applied work extends the baseline with the contextual layers relevant to each participant's sector.

**Output:** project geo-profile and a draft baseline map.

### Session 2 — Climate and risk screening *(28 September)*

Hazard and climate exposure screening over the project area, with applied work in sector breakout groups covering Agriculture and Water, Health and Education, and Energy and Transport.

**Output:** a short screening note and two or three candidate Earth Observation indicators.

### Session 3 — Monitoring, indicators and presentations *(1 October)*

Linking Earth Observation indicators to the Results Framework, and establishing a monitoring cadence and data refresh cycle, followed by participant case presentations.

**Output:** the final applied case, presented live.

---

## The applied case

Five slides, five minutes:

1. **The project** — title, country, stage, and the operational question being addressed
2. **Project footprint and baseline map**
3. **Climate and risk screening findings**
4. **Proposed Earth Observation indicators**, linked to the Results Framework
5. **How this will be used** in the project, and the next step
