## Learning objectives

By completing the Foundation Level programme, participants should be able to:

1. Understand the basic concepts of EO, GIS/Remote Sensing and geoinformatics relevant to IsDB operations
2. Identify and spatialize project locations, boundaries, assets and areas of influence
3. Use the EO-based Practical eToolkit and complementary geospatial tools to access and interpret project-relevant information
4. Strengthen quality at entry through project mapping, baseline development, site assessment and climate/risk screening
5. Identify EO-derived indicators that can complement PIASR and implementation monitoring
6. Apply baseline-to-end-line approaches relevant to PCR and PPER
7. Identify practical GEIDA use cases for their own ongoing or pipeline operations
8. Prepare and present an applied project case demonstrating how geospatial evidence can support an operational decision
