Planned for November 2026 · GPP Knowledge Week

!!! note "Programme in preparation"
    Batch 3 is the third cohort of the GEIDA Foundation Level course, planned to run during the GPP Knowledge Week in November 2026. Dates, format and registration details will be published here once confirmed.

---

## What to expect

Batch 3 will follow the same Foundation Level curriculum as [Batch 1](../batch-1/index.md) and [Batch 2](../batch-2/index.md): Earth Observation and geoinformatics fundamentals, spatialising an IsDB project across the project cycle, and hands-on work with the eToolkit and related web-based tools, using participants' own ongoing or pipeline projects.

No prior GIS, Remote Sensing or Earth Observation experience is required.

## Preparing in advance

Colleagues intending to join Batch 3 can begin with the materials already published on this site:

- The [preparatory resources](../resources/preparatory-batch-2.md) issued to Batch 2 participants
- The [Batch 1 session recordings and slide decks](../batch-1/index.md), which cover the full curriculum
