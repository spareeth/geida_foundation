# Preparatory Materials — Batch 2 (Part 1)

**Pre-training preparation · 1–10 September 2026**

---

Part 1 of the Batch 2 course is self-paced. These materials introduce the concepts and tools used on the in-person day, so that everyone begins from a common background and time in the room can be spent working on participants' own projects.

They remain available to all staff, and are the recommended starting point for colleagues preparing for [Batch 3](../batch-3/index.md).

!!! info "Suggested order"
    Work through the five sections below in sequence. Together they take approximately two to three hours.

!!! note "No prior experience required"
    The Foundation Level course assumes no background in GIS, Remote Sensing or Earth Observation. These materials are an introduction, not a prerequisite test.

## 5 — Select your project

Choose one specific ongoing or pipeline IsDB project that you are implementing, supervising or preparing, to use as your practical case throughout the training. Where available, bring:

- Location and coordinates, boundary or area of influence
- Key project components
- The Results Framework and its indicators
- Project start and expected completion dates

Submit your project details through the project data form:

[:material-form-select: **Project Data Submission Form**](https://forms.cloud.microsoft/Pages/ResponsePage.aspx?id=CA8x2HQVG0eId0PFAyJbGrgFJg6wpgZJskdpEwAADllUNU9ZNzVJSFUwUFJDSkJINTFRTjYxT0hZTy4u){ .md-button }

The same project carries through the in-person day and the [Part 3 online sessions](../batch-2/part-3.md), where it becomes your applied case.
